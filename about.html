About MediXon

Connecting people who have medical resources with those who need them most.

Our Mission

MediXon is a community driven platform built for Bangladesh. We believe a wheelchair, hospital bed, oxygen concentrator, or even a pack of unused medicine should never sit idle when someone else urgently needs it. MediXon bridges that gap connecting donors, borrowers, and buyers in one trusted interface.

Two Powerful Modules

🩺 Medical Equipment

Donate, swap, rent or sell wheelchairs, hospital beds, oxygen concentrators, nebulizers, monitors and more.

💊 Medicine Sharing

Share sealed, opened or partial medicine packs safely. Donate or sell at low prices to those in genuine need.

How It Works

  • Sign Up — Create your free account in seconds.
  • List Your Item — Upload equipment or medicine with photos and details.
  • Browse & Request — Search for what you need and send a request.
  • Chat — Message the owner directly, share photos, coordinate pickup.
  • Exchange — Donate, swap, rent or sell your choice.
